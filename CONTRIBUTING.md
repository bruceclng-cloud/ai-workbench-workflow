# Contributing

Issues, bug reports, compatibility reports and high-level suggestions are welcome.

External code or documentation contributions are **not accepted by default**. Please do not submit substantial pull requests unless the repository owner has first agreed in writing to accept the contribution and the applicable contribution/licensing terms.

This policy is intentional. AI Workbench Workflow may use dual licensing, commercial licensing, trademark protection, and potentially patent protection. Accepting unstructured third-party contributions can create uncertainty about copyright ownership, patent rights and the ability to license future versions consistently.

If the repository owner invites a contribution, the contributor must confirm that:

- they have the legal right to submit the material;
- the material does not knowingly include third-party code or confidential information that cannot be contributed;
- any AI-generated material has been reviewed and can lawfully be contributed;
- the contribution may be distributed by the repository owner under the project's current and future licensing models, subject to the written contribution terms agreed for that contribution;
- any patent-related obligations required by those written terms are satisfied.

Do not submit confidential inventions, unpublished patent-sensitive implementation details, employer-owned work, or material subject to incompatible licenses through a public issue or pull request.

A pull request being technically accepted by GitHub does not mean its licensing or ownership terms have been accepted by the project owner.
